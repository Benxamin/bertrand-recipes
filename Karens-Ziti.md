# Karen's Ziti

This is a baked pasta dish inspired by The Soprano's.

## Ingredients

Olive Oil
5 Garlic Cloves
1 bag Frozen Meatballs, thawed in fridge
1 can Tomatoes, crushed, large
1 jar Marinara, generic
Basil
Oregano
Salt
Pepper
1 lbs box Ziti

## Method

1. Over medium-low heat, sautee the garlic.

2. Add Tomatoes, and simmer for a while.

3. Add the Marinara, plus one jar of rinse wine and water, and Meatballs.

4. Bring to boil. Simmer for 30 minutes.

5. Add Ziti. Stir to coat.

6. Pour into greased 9 x 13 inch baking dish.

7. Dab with Ricotta. Top with shredded Mozzerella.

8. Cover with foil and bake at 350degF for 30 minutes.

9. Remove foil and bake for 15 minutes until the Mozzeralla cheese starts to brown.
