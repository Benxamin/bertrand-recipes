BLUEBERRY OATMEAL MUFFINS

INGREDIENTS
===========

DRY
===
1.5 c All-purpose flour
1/2 c Sugar
1/4 c Brown Sugar
3/4 c Rolled Oats
2 tsp Baking Powder
1/2 tsp Salt

WET
===
2 Eggs

3/4 c Milk
1/4 c Cooking Oil
OR 
1 c Heavy Whipping Cream

1 c Fresh or Frozen Blue Berries

METHOD
======
0. Preheat oven to 400º F.
1. Sift dry powdery ingredients into a large mixing bowl. Add Oats. Then, mix them until even texture. 
2. Add Milk into Large, Glass, 2-cup Measuring cup. Then add oil. Finally, crack an egg into the measuring cup after you have an even cup of liquid.
OR 
Beat two eggs in a large glass measuring cup. Then pour in the Cream.
3. Beat wet ingredients until there are a single even texture.
6. Pour wet ingredients into dry, and stir until blended evenly. 
7. Fold in the frozen berries. 
8. Grease two muffin pans (six muffins each). Spray Canola oil works best. Paper cups are okay, too.
9. Fill each muffin cup not quite full of batter.
10. Bake for 20 minutes, or until you smell them and they are puffy and golden brown on top.
