# Candied Walnuts

## Ingredients

1 Cup Brown Sugar
1 tsp Salt
1/2 Lemon Zest
2 Egg Whites
4 Cups Walnut Halves

## Method

1. Beat Egg Whites until stiff peaks form.
1. Fold in Brown Sugar, Salt and Lemon Zest
1. Once even, fold in Walnut Halves.
1. Spread coated nuts evenly on Parchment-covered cookie sheet.
1. Bake at 350degF for 15 minutes. Remove and let cool. Candy will harden as it cools.
