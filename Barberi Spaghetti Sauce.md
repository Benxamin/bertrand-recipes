# Mama Barberi's Sauce


## Ingredients

- 128 oz can marzano tomatoes pureed
- 3 large garlic cloves coarsely minced
- 1 large yellow onion chopped
- 1 lbs ground beef
- 2 Tbsp soy sauce
- 2 Tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp fennel seeds
- 1 tbs thyme
- 1/4 cup parsley flakes
- 1 tsp oregano
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/3 cup white wine

## Method

1. Saute onions and garlic in a little olive oil med low heat 10-15 min until soft
1. Increase heat to med, add beef and brown
1. Add soy sauce, parsley, thyme, oregano, salt, fennel, sugar. Stir until fragrant.
1. Add tomatoes, simmer covered 20 minutes
1. Add white wine, simmer 1-2 hours

Mangia!!
